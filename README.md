## 🙏 Special Poojas & Worship

The temple conducts various special worship programs throughout the year.

### 🌙 Pakshotsavams

* Sankatahara Chaturthi – Ganapathi Homa & Vijaya Ganapathi Aaradhana
* Panchami – Sri Varahi Mata Pradosha Aaradhana
* Shashti – Bala Subrahmanya Swamy Aaradhana
* Trayodashi – Basaveshwara Pradosha Aaradhana

### 🌕 Monthly Festivals

* Pournami Special Poojas
* Ardra Nakshatra Special Shiva Aaradhana
* Shani Trayodashi – Shanaiswara Swamy Aaradhana
* Masa Shivaratri – Special Shiva Aaradhana
* Amavasya – Varahi Devi Homa
